# Metronomics Pacing: Introduce-Then-Coach

**Last updated:** April 17, 2026 — aligned to the reconciled monthly view in `metronomics-deliverables.html` (Function Scorecards M5 before Market Map M6; Painted Picture pairs with 36-Month Forecast at M14; eNPS pairs with Skip-Level Reviews at M18).

## Rhythm

Every session follows the same three-block structure:

1. **Review block** (standing, every session from M2 onward): Quick confirm on the foundation. Is it still accurate, is anything shifting, are the numbers moving. Not rework, just alignment.
   - KFFM (from M1)
   - Functional Organisation Chart (from M1)
   - Functional Accountability Chart (from M1)
   - Profit/X (from M1)
   - Core Purpose (from M2)
   - BHAG (from M2)
   - Meeting Cadence (from M2)
   - QHAG + Sprint Lanes (from M3)
   - Monthly Forecast Review (from M3, part of Meeting Cadence)
   - Core Values (from M4)
   - A-Player Team Assessment (from M4)

2. **Coach block**: Review and refine last month's introduction. This is where the real coaching happens.

3. **Introduce block**: Introduce the next concept group. One new group per session. No stacking.

## Comparison to the old front-loaded pacing

| | Old (front-loaded) | New (introduce-then-coach) |
|---|---------|----------|
| Full deployment | 13 months | 23 months |
| M2 new introductions | 5 | 3 |
| Max new ★ in any month (post kick-off) | 5 | 3 (a few months at 2) |
| Average new ★ per month (post kick-off) | ~2.5 | ~1.2 |
| Deepening time (in a 3-year engagement) | 23 months | 13 months |

## Key Changes from the Old Front-Loaded Pacing

**M1 stays heavy.** The kick-off is a longer workshop and the KFFM system is one interconnected exercise. Can't separate those.

**M2 is the big fix.** The old M2 introduced Core Purpose, BHAG, Core Values, A-Player, and Meeting Cadence (five new frameworks on top of five evolving ones). New M2 introduces Core Purpose, BHAG, and Meeting Cadence — the "why / where / how-we-meet" trio from a single teaching arc.

**Core Values moves to M4.** Team has 3 months of working together before articulating how they work. Values are descriptive, not aspirational. They need shared experience to draw from.

**A-Player moves with Core Values (M4).** Arrives alongside Values discovery so the values axis has at least the beginning of real content, rather than pure gut-feel.

**Function Scorecards before Market Map (April 16 2026 reconciliation).** Function Scorecards is now M5, Market Map M6. Keeps the strategy cascade (Market Map → Core Customer → Attribution Map → Activity Fit Map → Swimlanes) unbroken from M6 through M10, and anchors Function Scorecards to the A-Player work that just closed at M4.

**Painted Picture and eNPS added (April 16 2026 reconciliation).** Painted Picture joins 36-Month Forecast at M14; eNPS pairs with Skip-Level Reviews at M18. Both fill cultural and human-measurement gaps in the old table.

**Cost: ten extra months of pacing.** 23 vs 13. In a 36-month engagement this is 13 months of true deepening (vs 23 in the front-loaded model) — but the deepening is much stronger because each foundation piece actually landed before the next one arrived.
