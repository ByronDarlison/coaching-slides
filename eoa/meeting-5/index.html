The rest of the meeting is about your plan, from your biggest goal down to this week's work. There are five levels.

At the top is your BHAG, your Big Hairy Audacious Goal. It's your long-term ambition, ten to thirty years out. Under it is the 3HAG, your Three-Year Highly Achievable Goal. Then the 1HAG for this year, and the QHAG for next quarter. At the bottom are your thirteen-week sprint lanes: the work, week by week.

The rule is simple. Each level is built from the one above it, and it directs the one below.

What each level holds

In the room: Keep this as the reference while members present.

Here's what goes into each level.

Your BHAG has no numbers. It's the ambition itself.

Your 3HAG holds year-three revenue and cash, and the widgets that get you there. It adds a statement of what the company will be, in your own words. Then three to five key capabilities, and what you want to be known for: in three years, what should people think when they hear your company's name?

Your 1HAG holds this year's revenue, cash, widgets and head count. It adds your annual priorities. Ideally three, never more than five, ranked with number one first.

Your QHAG holds the same numbers for the quarter, month by month, with three to five ranked company priorities.

And your sprint lanes give one lane for each quarterly priority, with the task that must get done each week, plus your widget forecast week by week.

Every number, every priority and every lane has one owner.
