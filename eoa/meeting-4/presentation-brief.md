## Communication job

By the end, each member should report actual progress, learn from peers, and commit to one measured next action. Sarah's walkthrough adds practical AI examples without replacing the group's accountability work.

## Problem and scope

Meeting 4 must fit a guest learning segment and the standing member work into five hours. The deck must preserve a confidential space for business updates and peer presentations after Sarah leaves. It must make prior commitments and monthly business figures observable, then coach the planning cascade each member prepared.

The deck does not ask members to complete Fatema's separate worksheets. Their EOA Prep document supplies the update, presentation preparation, and next commitment. The deck does not present Sarah's own materials, which she has not supplied, or invent details of her demonstration.

## Outcomes and success test

1. Each member reports the result of last month's one big thing and actual revenue, cash, and Profit/X against target.
2. Each member presents one prepared challenge in a 15-minute turn: five minutes to present, three for questions, and seven for peer experience.
3. Each member shows the link between their 3HAG, 1HAG, and quarterly sprint lanes, then records one next action tied to an Owner's Outcome measure.
4. The meeting ends by 6:00 PM with the next preparation deadline and meeting date clear.

Success is observable when the time chain reaches 300 minutes, all five members have an update and presentation turn, and the closing instructions produce a recorded next action. Live audience completion remains unverified until the meeting occurs.

## Central model

**Actuals → challenge → peer experience → planning cascade → next action.** The guest walkthrough supplies useful examples before this confidential sequence begins.
