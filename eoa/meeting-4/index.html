The BHAG, your Big Hairy Audacious Goal, is the company's long-term ambition, 10 to 30 years out. It is set where the Hedgehog's circles meet. It has no numbers.

The 3HAG, the Three-Year Highly Achievable Goal, is the next three-year step toward the BHAG. It holds year-three revenue and cash, the widgets that get there, a statement of what the company will be, 3 to 5 key capabilities, and what it wants to be known for.

The 1HAG, the One-Year Highly Achievable Goal, is built from the 3HAG. It holds the year's revenue, cash, widgets and head count, and ideally 3, up to 5 ranked annual priorities, number one first, each with one owner. It directs the QHAG.

The QHAG, the Quarterly Highly Achievable Goal, is built from the 1HAG. It holds the quarter's revenue, cash, widgets and head count, month by month, and ideally 3, up to 5 ranked company priorities, number one first, each with one owner. It directs the 13-Week Sprint Lanes.

The sprint lanes give one lane for each QHAG priority, the task that must be done each week to hit the priority by quarter end, with the quarter's widget forecast week by week.

Each level is built from the one above it and directs the one below.
