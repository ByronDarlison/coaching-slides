Owner's Outcome Guardrails

The edges you protect on the way to the goal.

  • What failure looks like. The picture of this business failing you even if the numbers hit. Not what you are afraid of. What you refuse to walk into on purpose.
  • Non-Negotiables. The rules you will not break even if it costs you money or growth. Time, health, family, the kind of work you do, the kind of people you work with. If you have broken one before, it is not a non-negotiable.
  • Cash Floor. The number below which you start making decisions from anxiety instead of clarity. Not the number that sounds reasonable. The real one.
  • Home Alignment. If you have a partner, do they know what you want from this business, and do they agree? Misalignment at home will override everything else you build.
