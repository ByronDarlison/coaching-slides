# Security and privacy

The published decks are static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and image files. They should not contain secrets, client information, private meeting notes, or credentials.

## Before publishing

- Inspect links and embedded resources.
- Confirm that examples do not identify a client without permission.
- Keep access tokens and service credentials out of source files.
- Confirm that third-party media can be publicly redistributed.

## Reporting a concern

Do not open a public issue if the report would expose private information or a credential. Contact Byron Darlison through [darlison.com](https://www.darlison.com/) and identify the affected deck or file.
